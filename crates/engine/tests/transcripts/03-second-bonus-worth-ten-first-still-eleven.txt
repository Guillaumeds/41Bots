title: A partnership's second best-trump trick is worth 10 while another partnership's first is still worth 11
story: Three hands of the same game, showing the bonus bookkeeping of rule 28: each partnership's first best-trump trick in a game is worth 11 (5 + 6), every later one 10 (5 + 5).
story: Hand 1 (seat 5 deals, diamonds trumps): team 1 (seats 1 and 5) wins the trick holding the best trump played (the J of diamonds); its first bonus, +6, counted at the end of the hand. Scores 0, 16, 5, 10 (teams 0 to 3).
story: Hand 2 (seat 6 deals, clubs trumps): team 1 again wins the best-trump trick (the 5 of clubs); its second bonus this game is only +5, counted at once (the 5 of trumps was played). Scores 5, 36, 5, 15.
story: Hand 3 (seat 7 deals, spades trumps): team 3 (seats 3 and 7) wins the best-trump trick (the Joker); it is team 3's first bonus, so +6 in full, whatever team 1 has had. Scores 5, 36, 15, 36.
story: In every hand the ace of trumps is undealt, so nobody robs. Only the bonus lines and the scores are checked; the other cards are any legal plays.
