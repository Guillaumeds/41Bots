title: A turned 5 of trumps is robbed by the holder of the ace, who then plays it for the bonus at once
story: Seat 6 deals and the 5 of diamonds is turned: diamonds are trumps. Seat 2 holds the ace of diamonds (and the ace of clubs).
story: Seat 7 leads trick 1; seats 7, 0 and 1 play without robbing. When seat 2's turn comes it must rob (rule 20, H2): it takes the 5 of diamonds and burns one card face down.
story: Any card except the ace of trumps may be burned (rule 21, H3): the ace of clubs (a plain ace here) and the 5 of diamonds itself are allowed, the ace of diamonds is not; seat 2 burns the 9 of spades.
story: Seat 2 then plays the 5 of diamonds, which wins trick 1. Because the 5 of trumps was played, its trick is the best-trump trick and the bonus counts at once: team 2 (seats 2 and 6) has 11 after trick 1.
story: The other tricks are ordinary; no bonus is awarded at the end of the hand. Final scores of the hand: 5,5,16,5.
