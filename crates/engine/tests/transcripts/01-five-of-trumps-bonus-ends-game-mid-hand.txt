title: The 5 of trumps bonus counts at once and ends the game in the middle of a hand
story: Hand 1 (seat 0 deals, clubs trumps) only builds the score: team 2 (seats 2 and 6) takes four tricks and its first best-trump bonus (seat 2 plays the 5 of clubs in trick 1), so the scores are 5, 0, 26, 0 (teams 0 to 3).
story: Hand 2: seat 1 deals and the 8 of diamonds is turned, so diamonds are trumps. The ace and J of diamonds are undealt, so nobody robs.
story: Seat 2 leads trick 1 with the Joker and wins it: team 2 has 31.
story: Seat 2 then leads the 5 of diamonds and wins trick 2: 5 for the trick makes 36, and because the 5 of trumps was played its bonus counts the moment the trick is won (rule 29).
story: Team 2 already had a bonus this game (hand 1), so this one is worth 5 extra (rule 28: 10 for the trick): 36 + 5 = 41. Team 2 wins the game at once, in the middle of the hand (rule 32); tricks 3 to 5 are not played.
story: The game-end reason is "immediate bonus": the trick alone left team 2 on 36, and the bonus took it to 41.
