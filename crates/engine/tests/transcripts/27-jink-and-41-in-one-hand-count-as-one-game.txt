title: A jink that would also reach 41 with the end-of-hand bonus counts as one game, not two
story: Hand 1 (seat 3 deals, clubs trumps) only builds the score: team 2 (seats 2 and 6) takes two tricks and no bonus, so the scores are 11, 5, 10, 5.
story: Hand 2: seat 4 deals and turns the 9 of spades. The 5 and the ace of spades are undealt: nobody robs and the 5 is never played, so the best-trump bonus would be awarded at the end of the hand.
story: Seat 6 holds the J of spades, the Joker, the A♥, the king and the queen of spades. It wins trick 1 with the J and then leads its other trumps, winning every trick: team 2 has all five tricks and 10 + 25 = 35 points.
story: After trick 5 the jink is checked before the end-of-hand bonus (RULES "End checks"), so team 2 wins the game by the jink with 35. The bonus for the J of spades (+6, which would have made 41) is never awarded: the jink and the 41 are one game, not two.
story: The match goes on: hand 3 is the first hand of game 2, dealt by seat 5, with all scores back to nought.
