title: A turned 5 of trumps with the ace undealt stays on the table; the bonus goes to the J at the end of the hand
story: Seat 7 deals and the 5 of clubs is turned: clubs are trumps. The ace of clubs is in the undealt pack, so nobody can rob: the 5 stays face up on the table, out of play, for the whole hand (rule 23).
story: In trick 1 every player plays without robbing, so each in turn proves they lack the ace; after the eighth card the ace is known to be undealt.
story: Seat 3 wins trick 1 with the J of clubs, the best trump in play. The trick scores 5 only: because the 5 of trumps is never played, the bonus is decided at the end of the hand (rule 30).
story: At the end of the hand the J of clubs is still the highest trump played, so team 3 (seats 3 and 7) gets +6. Final scores of the hand: 5,0,5,21.
story: Compare with transcript 06, where the turned 5 is robbed and played and its bonus counts at once.
