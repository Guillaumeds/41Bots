title: When only low trumps are played, the end-of-hand bonus goes to the best of them (the A♥, then a black 2)
story: There are 15 trumps (14 when hearts are trumps) and only 13 cards are out of play, so at least two trumps always reach the players when hearts are not trumps; here the deals leave exactly two.
story: Hand 1: seat 1 deals and the 10 of spades is turned. The other spades and the Joker are undealt, so the only trumps in play are the ace of hearts (a trump in every hand, rule 7) and the 3 of spades.
story: Seat 0 wins trick 1 with the 3 of spades and seat 3 wins trick 3 with the ace of hearts. The 5 is never played, so at the end of the hand the bonus goes to the trick with the highest trump played, the ace of hearts (rule 30): team 3 gets +6. Scores 10,0,10,11.
story: Hand 2: seat 2 deals and the 9 of clubs is turned. All other clubs, the Joker and the ace of hearts are undealt, so the only trumps in play are the 2 and the 10 of clubs.
story: Seat 1 wins trick 3 with the 10 of clubs, the lowest trump of all, and seat 4 wins trick 4 with the 2 of clubs. In a black trump suit the 2 beats the 10 (rule 6), so the 2 of clubs is the best trump played and team 0 gets the end-of-hand bonus, +6. Scores 21,5,15,21.
