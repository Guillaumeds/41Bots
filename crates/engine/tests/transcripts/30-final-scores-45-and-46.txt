title: Scores can pass 41: a team on 40 takes a trick (45); a team on 36 takes the 5-of-trumps trick (46)
story: Six hands, two games, showing that a final score can go past 41 (the game is won on reaching 41 or more).
story: Game 1: in hands 1 and 2 team 1 (seats 1 and 5) takes four tricks each time but never the best-trump trick, so it stands on 40 with no bonus (scores 0,40,0,21). In hand 3 it wins trick 1: 45, and wins game 1 at once (0,45,0,21).
story: Game 2 starts from nought. In hand 4 team 3 (seats 3 and 7) takes four tricks and its first bonus (26), and in hand 5 two more tricks: 36 (scores 5,16,5,36).
story: In hand 6 seat 7 plays the 5 of spades in trick 1 and wins it. The trick takes team 3 to 41, and the 5-of-trumps bonus counts with it at once: team 3's second bonus of the game, +5, so the final score is 46.
story: Because the trick alone already made 41, the game-end reason is "trick" (not "immediate bonus"). Final scores of game 2: 5,16,5,46. Transcript 31 shows the 47 that the flat 11-every-time rule would give.
