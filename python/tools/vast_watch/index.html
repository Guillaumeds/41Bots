How the numbers are made. Every listing's cost per 10¹² nodes [positions the exact solver examines; our work is counted in them] = price per hour ÷ (usable threads × measured speed per thread × 3600) × 10¹². Speed per thread is from our own bench runs for the CPU models we have measured (green measured), from a sibling model (grey family), or from the speed model (orange estimate). It is then adjusted for how small the solver's memory tables would be on that machine's RAM. Usable threads are 95.8% of the advertised ones. The three categories rank only machines our launcher can rent (whole, verified, reliability above 0.90, network at most $0.10/GB) with enough threads. "Fast" means at least 1 billion nodes per second. "Best balanced" is the lowest cost × time. Search every few seconds is the program's; keep it running to keep the records.
